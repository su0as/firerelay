# FireRelay: one fire record from MODIS to VIIRS

FireRelay harmonizes 25 years of NASA FIRMS active-fire hotspots (MODIS 2000 onward, VIIRS 2012 onward) into one continuous record in **NOAA-20 VIIRS 375 m equivalent** units. It turns that record into a burning-activity calendar for any area on Earth.

Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, *Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots*.

- **Live app:** https://firerelay.vercel.app (method and validation: https://firerelay.vercel.app/method.html)
- **Demo:** [30-second narrated video](https://youtu.be/3cyQH2OKh7o) ([mp4](https://firerelay.vercel.app/demo/firerelay-demo.mp4)) · [7-slide deck](https://firerelay.vercel.app/demo/firerelay-slides.pdf)
- **App:** static site in `web/` (Leaflet map, NASA GIBS basemap, no server, no API keys in the browser). The data tiles in `web/data/tiles` and `web/data/weekly` (about 70 MB, 1,678 files) are generated by the pipeline. They are served from the live site and not committed here.
- **Pipeline:** `pipeline/` (Node.js, no native dependencies)

## Why

- **The two sensor families see different numbers of hotspots for the same fires.** Worldwide, S-NPP VIIRS records about 6 times as many hotspots as Aqua MODIS, and about 12 times as many as Terra MODIS.
- **Stitching the records naively creates a false jump in 2012.** Using VIIRS alone throws away 11 years of history.
- **The sensors are ending.** MODIS is near the end of its life, and S-NPP stopped delivering data on 2 November 2026.
- **The record has to be carried forward by NOAA-20 and NOAA-21.** FireRelay therefore uses NOAA-20 as its reference unit. Every new month is an observation, and only the frozen MODIS archive is modelled.

## Method in one paragraph

Every sensor passes the same filters:
- FIRMS `type 0`, presumed vegetation fire
- nominal or high confidence

Detections are counted on a 0.25° grid by month and on a 1° grid by week.

The record is built in three eras:
- **April 2018 onward:** observed NOAA-20.
- **February 2012 to March 2018:** S-NPP, multiplied by a per-cell NOAA-20/S-NPP ratio (global value about 0.99).
- **Before February 2012:** Terra and Aqua are each multiplied by a per-cell, per-calendar-month ratio fitted against S-NPP over February 2012 to December 2019, when both MODIS platforms were still in nominal orbit. The two estimates are averaged.

All ratios are ratio-of-sums estimators. They are shrunk toward their parent (0.25° cell → 1° block by calendar month → 1° block → 5° tile → globe) with a pseudo-count λ chosen on held-out data.

## Validation (held-out years)

The test fits ratios on 2012–2016 and predicts S-NPP counts for 2017–2019 from MODIS only.

- **Median absolute error of 1° monthly totals:** about 34% for FireRelay, versus 43% for one global ratio and 87% for raw MODIS.
- **Same test, yearly totals:** 14% for 1° blocks, 10% for 5° tiles and 12% for the 12 named areas in the app (raw MODIS: about 88%). Whole-globe yearly totals: within 1%.
- **80% ranges:** cover 76% of 1° monthly hold-out totals and 80% of named-area totals in stable years, and about 72% under MODIS orbit drift (2020–2025). They are slightly too narrow.
- **Method contest (same hold-out years, `pipeline/validate_methods.mjs`):** 1° monthly error 34% for FireRelay vs 43% one global ratio, 48% quantile mapping, 61% log-log regression, 87% raw MODIS. Yearly 5° tiles: 10% vs 26%, 106%, 134%, 88%.
- **80% ranges recalibrated:** widened ×1.2 (fitted on Test A) so they hold 80% of Test A and 77% of Test B (drift years) monthly totals.
- **In the app:** every area shows its own measured hold-out error, a naive-stitch vs FireRelay trend check, and a typical next-3-months outlook (history, not a forecast).
- **Weakest region:** Riau is overestimated in every hold-out year (+21% to +134%), so MODIS-era peatland estimates are the least certain part of the record.
- **Log correlation at 5° monthly:** 0.92.
- **Peak burning month:** same as VIIRS in about 77% of region-years.
- **Noise floor:** the two VIIRS instruments disagree by about 17% at the same scale.

Full tables, a test against the 2020–2025 orbit-drift years, and the uncertainty model are on `web/method.html`. The numbers there are generated from `web/data/validation.json`.

## Rebuild

```bash
# 1. yearly FIRMS archives (no login needed), streamed and aggregated; zips deleted as they go
pipeline/fetch_archive.sh modis       2000 2025 ./dl ./agg
pipeline/fetch_archive.sh viirs-snpp  2012 2025 ./dl ./agg
pipeline/fetch_archive.sh viirs-jpss1 2018 2025 ./dl ./agg
# 2. current year from the FIRMS area API (free MAP_KEY), standard data where available, NRT after
MAP_KEY=... pipeline/fetch_api.sh VIIRS_NOAA20_SP 2026-01-01 2026-07-31 | node pipeline/aggregate.mjs viirs-jpss1 2026 ./agg2026
# 3. calibrate, validate, write tiles
SEASONAL=1 LAMBDAS=30,100,300 LAST_DATE=2026-11-15 node --max-old-space-size=6000 pipeline/build.mjs ./agg,./agg2026 web
node pipeline/build_presets.mjs web/data/geo/presets.json
# 4. score the same hold-out tests at block, tile, named-area and global scales
SEASONAL=1 node --max-old-space-size=6000 pipeline/validate_scales.mjs ./agg,./agg2026 web web/data/validation_scales.json
SEASONAL=1 node --max-old-space-size=8000 pipeline/validate_methods.mjs ./agg,./agg2026 web web/data/validation_methods.json
node pipeline/serve.mjs web 8765
```

## Limitations

- **Hotspots are detections, not burned area.** Clouds and smoke hide fires.
- **The MODIS-era estimates assume the 2012–2019 relationship between the sensors held earlier.** They carry an 80% uncertainty band.
- **Areas are built from 0.25° cells.**
- **The latest months are near-real-time data.**

This is not an official NASA product.
