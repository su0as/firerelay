When and where does it burn, compared with the last 25 years?

Satellites have tracked fire hotspots since 2000, but the record is split: MODIS (2000 onward, 1 km pixels) and VIIRS (2012 onward, 375 m pixels) count fires differently, and MODIS is being retired. FireRelay calibrates the whole record to one unit, NOAA-20 VIIRS-equivalent hotspots, so every month from 2001 to today can be compared directly.

3 The record, sensor by sensor

Counts are hotspots (nominal and high confidence, vegetation fires only) in NOAA-20 VIIRS 375 m units, summed over the outlined 0.25° cells, weighted by how much of each cell lies inside your area. Months before February 2012 are estimated from MODIS and carry an 80% uncertainty band. How it works and how well it works.

Data: NASA FIRMS (MODIS Collection 6.1 MCD14ML; VIIRS 375 m VNP14IMG, VJ114IMG), NASA GIBS Blue Marble basemap, geoBoundaries. Not an official NASA product. Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, “Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots”.
